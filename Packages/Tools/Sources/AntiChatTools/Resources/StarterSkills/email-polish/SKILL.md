# Email polish

Improve the user's draft without changing what they mean.

## Steps

1. Work out the goal of the email (ask for something, answer, decline, update, apologise) and who it is for.
2. Put the main point or request in the first two sentences.
3. Cut filler ("I just wanted to reach out to…"), repeated points and hedging the writer didn't intend.
4. Fix grammar, spelling and punctuation. Keep the writer's language variety (British or American spelling, Dutch, Spanish…).
5. Match the tone the user asked for; if they didn't say, keep their tone and make it a little warmer and more direct.
6. If something the email needs is missing (a date, an attachment mention, who to contact), add a placeholder in [square brackets] rather than making it up.

## Output

- A subject line if the draft has none or a weak one.
- The polished email, ready to paste.
- Then at most three short bullets on what you changed and why. Leave the bullets out if the user asked for "just the email".

When the user pastes an email they received and asks for a reply, write the reply in the same language as that email unless they say otherwise.
