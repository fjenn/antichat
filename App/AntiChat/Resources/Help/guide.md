# AntiChat short guide

## Getting started
Type in the box at the bottom and press Return. AntiChat answers with a model that runs on this Mac. Start a new chat with ⌘N. Your chats are in the sidebar, and **Settings** is at the bottom of it.

## What AntiChat can do
Chat is always there. Voice, Images and Video are extras you turn on in **Settings › Capabilities**. Each shows its download size first, and AntiChat only offers what fits your Mac. Click **+** in the chat box to add photos and files, or to make an image, a video or a document.

## Apps and skills
**Settings › Connections** lists apps such as Gmail and Google Calendar. Click **+** next to an app and sign in in your browser. You can also ask in a chat, for example "connect my Gmail", and a Connect card appears. **Settings › Skills** has ready-made skills that teach AntiChat one kind of task.

## Evil Mode
An unfiltered mode for adults. Turn on Evil Mode in **Settings › Capabilities**, confirm you are 18 or older, and wait for its download. Then start a new chat and pick **Evil** in the chat box. Evil chats have a red frame. One thing is blocked in every mode: sexual images or video involving minors.

## Privacy
Your chats, pictures, files and models stay on this Mac. In **Settings › Privacy & Data**, **Offline only** pauses everything that uses the internet: web search, connections, updates and downloads. An incognito chat (**File › New Incognito Chat**) is never saved.

## Memory
AntiChat can remember things you tell it and use them in later chats. In **Settings › Personalization** you can see, change or delete what it remembers, and say how you like your answers.

## Disk space
Models are large. **Settings › Privacy & Data** shows what uses space under Storage, and **Clean up** clears caches and unfinished downloads. To remove Voice, Images or Video, turn it off in **Settings › Capabilities** and choose to delete its files.

## Uninstall
**AntiChat › Uninstall AntiChat…** removes the app and everything it keeps. **Help › How to Uninstall** lists every folder if you prefer to do it by hand.

## Troubleshooting
- A download stopped: **Settings › Capabilities**, then Retry. Downloads resume.
- Slow answers: close other heavy apps. AntiChat switches to a smaller model when memory is tight.
- Anything else: **Help › Report a Problem…** builds a report you can read before sending. You can attach screenshots and files.
