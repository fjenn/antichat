# AntiChat short guide

## Getting started
Type in the box at the bottom and press Return. AntiChat answers with a model that runs on this Mac. Start a new chat with ⌘N; your chats are in the sidebar.

## Packs
Chat is always there. Voice, Photo and Video are packs you can add or remove in **Settings › Capabilities**. Each shows its download size first, and AntiChat only offers what fits your Mac.

## Evil Mode
An unfiltered mode for adults. Turn it on in **Settings › Evil Mode** after confirming you are 18 or older; Evil chats have a red frame. One thing is blocked in every mode: sexual images or video involving minors.

## Privacy
Your chats, pictures, files and models stay on this Mac. Online features (web search, connections, updates) are listed in **Settings › Privacy & Data**, where **Offline only** turns all of them off.

## Disk space
Models are large. **Settings › Storage** shows what each pack uses, cleans up old versions and can move the Models folder to another drive.

## Uninstall
**AntiChat › Uninstall AntiChat…** removes the app and everything it keeps. **Help › How to Uninstall** lists every folder if you prefer to do it by hand.

## Troubleshooting
- A download stopped: **Settings › Capabilities**, then Retry. Downloads resume.
- Slow answers: close other heavy apps; AntiChat switches to a smaller model when memory is tight.
- Anything else: **Help › Report a Problem…** builds a report you can read before sending.
