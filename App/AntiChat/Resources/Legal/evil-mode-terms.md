- Evil Mode is for adults (18+). By turning it on you confirm you are 18 or older.
- It has near-zero refusals and allows adult content. It can be wrong, offensive or explicit; you are responsible for how you use it.
- **One block in every mode:** AntiChat checks generated pictures and video on your Mac and blocks sexual content involving minors. That is the only filter; it runs locally and sends nothing.
- Creating sexual imagery of real people without their consent is forbidden.
