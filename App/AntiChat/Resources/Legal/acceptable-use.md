You may use AntiChat for anything lawful. You may not use it to:
- create, request or share sexual content involving minors (blocked in every mode; see the [Evil Mode terms](https://antichat.ai/legal/evil-mode-terms/));
- create sexual imagery of real people without their consent;
- harass, threaten or defraud people, or break the law where you are.

Report abuse: see [abuse contact](https://antichat.ai/legal/abuse/).
