# AntiChat 0.0.7

- Connect over 1,500 apps: a new Connections page you can search, with one click to connect, plus a Connect card right in the chat. Anything that sends or changes something asks first.
- A skills library: about 180 ready-made skills to install, kept up to date, with one-click updates.
- Settings is simpler and lives inside the main window: six pages instead of thirteen.
- Memory works by itself: AntiChat remembers lasting things you tell it and shows what it saved, with Undo.
- Better answers: long replies are no longer cut short, follow-up questions remember what was just found, video works as soon as its download finishes, and Evil Mode has a calmer look and sets itself up reliably.
